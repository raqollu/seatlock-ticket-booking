A seat. A plan. A night.

  1. Find your experience.

    Choose a show and check live seat availability.

  2. Make a little room.

    Select up to 8 seats. Holding them reserves your selection for up to 15 minutes, or until the show starts.

  3. You’re on the list.

    Confirm your booking before the timer runs out. This is a demo; there is no payment step.

Changed your mind? Release your seats from My bookings. If a hold expires, the seats become available again automatically.
